PRIVACY NOTICE

How Pulse Chess handles data

Last updated September 2026

Account data

When you create an account, Pulse Chess stores your username, email address, password hash or Google account identifier, country code when available, profile settings, rating and game record. Passwords are hashed with Argon2 and are not stored as plain text.

Games, friends and messages

Rated match results and rating changes are stored so match history and leaderboards can work. Match chat is relayed during the game and is not intentionally stored in the matches table. Friend relationships and direct messages are stored so your friends list and private conversations remain available when you return.

Cookies and country flags

Pulse Chess uses a strictly necessary session cookie when you sign in so the server can keep you authenticated. The site currently does not use advertising cookies. Your cookie banner choice is stored locally in your browser. Small country-flag images are loaded from FlagCDN when a country badge is shown.

Security

Pulse Chess uses server-side sessions, request rate limiting, security headers, parameterized database queries, server-side chess validation and input limits. No online service can promise perfect security, so do not reuse your Pulse Chess password on other services.

TERMS OF USE

Rules for using Pulse Chess

Last updated September 2026

Accounts

Use accurate account information, keep your credentials secure, and do not impersonate another person. Accounts are for the person who created them.

Fair play

Do not use chess engines, outside move assistance, automation, exploits, or another person during rated multiplayer games. Built-in practice bots and post-game analysis are allowed because they are part of Pulse Chess.

Community conduct

Do not harass, threaten, spam, impersonate, or post hateful or abusive usernames, bios, chat messages, or direct messages. Pulse Chess may remove content, restrict messaging, or suspend access when needed to protect the service or other users.

Your content

You keep ownership of content you create. By posting a username, bio, message, or other profile content, you give Pulse Chess permission to store and display it only as needed to operate the service.

Games and ratings

Ratings, game records, and results may change because of bugs, abuse prevention, moderation, or future rating-system changes. Pulse Chess does not guarantee uninterrupted service or error-free gameplay.

Account restrictions

Access may be restricted for cheating, abuse, security attacks, spam, repeated rule violations, or misuse of the service. You can delete your own account from Settings.

Changes

These terms may be updated as Pulse Chess grows. Continued use after an updated version is posted means you agree to the updated terms.

COOKIE NOTICE

Cookies & local preferences

Last updated September 2026

Necessary session cookie

When you sign in, Pulse Chess uses a necessary session cookie so the server can keep you authenticated. Rejecting optional preferences does not disable this required login cookie.

Optional browser storage

If you accept optional preferences, Pulse Chess can remember settings such as board theme, sound, motion, and volume in local storage. These preferences are stored in your browser, not used for advertising.

No ad tracking

Pulse Chess does not currently use advertising cookies or cross-site advertising trackers.

FAIR PLAY

Play your own moves

Last updated September 2026

Rated games

During a rated game, choose moves yourself. Do not use Stockfish, another chess engine, analysis software, move suggestions, automation, or another person to decide your moves.

Practice & review

Using Pulse Chess bots is allowed in bot mode. Stockfish review is allowed after a game is complete. Neither feature affects your rated multiplayer result.

Exploits

Do not intentionally abuse disconnects, clock bugs, duplicated requests, modified clients, or server vulnerabilities to change a result or rating.

Reports

Use the report button for suspected cheating, harassment, spam, or abusive profile content. Reports are stored for moderation review.
